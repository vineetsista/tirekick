Identify visible exterior damage in this photograph: dents, creases, scratches
deep enough to catch light, cracked or missing trim, and panel gaps that are
visibly uneven against the panel beside them.

Box each one separately. Two dents are two findings.

Things that are not damage, and that this pass gets wrong if you let it:

- Shadows, especially along body lines and under mirrors and handles
- Reflections of trees, buildings, sky, and the photographer
- Water droplets, dried water spots, road film, mud
- Trim, badges, sensors, antennas, and factory seams that belong there
- Wrapped or matte finishes, which read as texture defects but are deliberate

If the light or the angle means you cannot tell a dent from a reflection, that is
a case for saying so in the confidence basis, or for not reporting it at all.

Severity is about repair, not appearance: `minor` for something a buyer would
live with or fix cheaply, `major` for panel work or a respray, `critical` only
for damage that is visibly beyond cosmetic. Most exterior damage is minor.
