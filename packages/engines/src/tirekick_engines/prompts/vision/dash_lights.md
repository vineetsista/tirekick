Identify warning lamps that are illuminated on this instrument cluster.

Name each lamp you can identify - check engine, oil pressure, coolant
temperature, battery, ABS, traction control, tyre pressure, and so on - and box
each one.

Report only lamps that are lit. A dark lamp is not a finding.

Two things to weigh, and they matter more here than anywhere else in this
analysis. If the photograph was taken during the ignition self-test, before the
engine started, every lamp on the cluster illuminates and none of it means
anything. Say so if the cluster looks like a self-test. And a cluster with no
lamps lit at all, in a car that is not running, tells you nothing either.

Name the lamp. Do not interpret what it implies about the vehicle's condition,
and do not estimate a repair from it. An airbag or brake warning lamp is named
and described, and nothing further is said about what it means.
