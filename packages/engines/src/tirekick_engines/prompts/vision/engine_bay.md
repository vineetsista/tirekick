Describe what is visible in this engine bay.

Report: wet or dark residue on or below components, crusted deposits around hose
joints and gasket edges, corrosion on battery terminals, coolant staining, a
cracked or perished belt or hose where the crack is actually visible, and
components that look conspicuously newer or older than everything around them.

Box each observation.

You cannot tell from a photograph whether a leak is active, what the fluid is, or
where it came from. Residue may be a current leak, a repaired one nobody cleaned
up, or something spilled during a top-up. Say that plainly rather than picking
one.

A bay that has just been steam-cleaned hides everything this pass looks for. If
it looks freshly cleaned, that is worth reporting on its own - not as an
accusation, but because it means the absence of residue tells the buyer nothing.

Do not assess brake components, brake lines, the master cylinder, steering
components, suspension struts, or any structural member, even when they are
plainly in frame. If something adverse is visible in those regions, describe what
you see and attach no judgment to it.
