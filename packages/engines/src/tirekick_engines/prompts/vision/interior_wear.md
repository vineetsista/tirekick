Describe visible wear inside the cabin: the driver's seat bolster, the steering
wheel rim, the pedal rubbers, the shift knob, the armrest, the headliner, and the
carpet at the driver's heel.

Box what you describe.

Wear is evidence about how much the car has been used, and it is one of the few
things a photograph shows honestly. Report what you see - shine, cracking,
splitting, polish on the wheel, worn-through pedal rubber - without estimating
mileage from it in this pass.

Note anything that suggests water has been inside: staining with a tide line,
silt in the seat rails or under the carpet edge, visible corrosion on seat frames
or belt anchors, or a fogged instrument lens. Describe what is visible. Do not
conclude that a vehicle was flooded.

Aftermarket seat covers, floor mats, and steering wheel covers hide exactly the
areas this pass reads. If they are fitted, say so - what is hidden matters more
than what is shown.
