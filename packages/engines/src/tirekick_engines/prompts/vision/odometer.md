Read the odometer in this photograph.

Report the digits exactly as displayed and the units if shown. Do not round, do
not reformat, and do not infer a digit that is cut off, glared out, or blurred.

Box the reading itself, tightly.

If any digit is not legible, the reading is not legible. Say so and report no
number. A number with one wrong digit is out by a factor of ten, and the buyer
will act on it. This is the pass where a confident guess does the most damage.

Note if you can see a trip meter rather than the total, if the display is a
segment LCD with digits that could be partially unlit, or if the cluster appears
to be aftermarket or replaced.
