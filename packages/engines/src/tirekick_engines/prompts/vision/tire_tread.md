Assess the tyre in this photograph.

Report: visible tread depth as a band rather than a single figure, uneven wear
across the tread face, cupping or feathering, sidewall cracking or bulging,
kerbing damage to the wheel, and the DOT date code if it is legible.

Box what you report.

Tread depth from a photograph is an estimate and you must write it as one. The
angle, the lighting, and whether the photograph shows the outer shoulder or the
centre rib all move the answer. A band with a stated basis is honest; a number to
one decimal place is not.

Uneven wear is the finding worth catching. Wear on one shoulder only, or on both
shoulders with a good centre, or a scalloped pattern, points at alignment,
inflation, or suspension - so report the pattern you can see and let a mechanic
say why. Do not diagnose the cause.

Note the brand and whether the tyres appear to match, if you can see it. Four
different brands is a thing a buyer should know.
